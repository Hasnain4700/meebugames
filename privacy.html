This policy applies to Meebu Games titles, mobile applications, related player services, and meebugames.com. A particular game or platform may provide an additional notice where its features require one.

01 / Introduction

Who this policy is for

Meebu Games ("Meebu", "we", "us", or "our") creates and publishes interactive entertainment. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you install, access, or communicate with one of our games, applications, websites, or support channels (together, the "Services").

By using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the affected Service.

02 / Information we collect

Information depends on how you play

2.1 Information collected automatically

When you use a game or application, we or our service providers may receive technical and gameplay information such as:

  • device model, operating system, app version, language, and time zone;
  • IP address and approximate country or region;
  • advertising identifiers, such as a Google Advertising ID or another platform identifier;
  • gameplay events, progress, scores, session information, and feature interactions; and
  • performance data, error logs, and crash diagnostics.

2.2 In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the relevant platform store, such as Google Play, the Apple App Store, Samsung Galaxy Store, Amazon Appstore, or another authorised marketplace. Meebu does not receive or store your full card or bank details. We may receive a receipt, transaction identifier, product purchased, transaction status, and country or region so we can verify the purchase and provide support.

2.3 Support and direct contact

If you email us or submit an enquiry, we receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, game or device details, and the history of your request. Please do not send sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your issue.

2.4 Website information

When you visit our website, the hosting and network providers that deliver it may process ordinary request data such as your IP address, browser type, requested page, and access time for security, reliability, and diagnostics.

03 / Advertising & mediation

How ad-supported games work

Some Meebu games may display third-party advertising. Depending on the title and platform, advertising or mediation providers may include Unity Ads, AppLovin MAX, Google AdMob, and Mintegral. These partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device details, ad interactions, and general location to deliver ads, measure performance, apply your consent choices, and prevent fraud.

Each provider acts under its own privacy policy. The providers used by a particular title may change as we improve or distribute that title. You can limit personalised advertising or reset/delete your advertising identifier through your device or platform settings where those controls are available.

04 / How we use information

Why we process data

We use information only where it helps us operate, secure, understand, or improve the Services. Common purposes include:

  • running the game correctly on your device and delivering requested features;
  • saving or synchronising progress where a title supports those functions;
  • verifying purchases and restoring purchased content;
  • answering support requests and communicating about service issues;
  • finding bugs, preventing abuse, improving stability, and balancing gameplay;
  • measuring audiences and advertising performance; and
  • meeting legal obligations and enforcing our agreements.

Where required by law, we rely on an appropriate legal basis such as performing a contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Services, compliance with legal duties, or your consent.

05 / Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to young children

Unless a game says otherwise in its store listing or an age-specific notice, our Services are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child in a way that requires parental consent without first obtaining that consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to Meebu, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

06 / Sharing & retention

Limited sharing, limited time

We may share relevant information with platform operators, hosting and cloud providers, analytics and crash-reporting services, advertising partners, customer-support providers, professional advisers, or legal authorities when required. Providers may use information only for the contracted service or as separately explained in their own notices.

We do not sell personal information for money. If applicable law treats certain advertising disclosures as a "sale" or "sharing", you may exercise the opt-out choices described in the Your rights section.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Services, support users, secure our systems, comply with law, or resolve disputes. Retention periods vary by data type, game feature, provider, and legal requirement. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

07 / Your rights & choices

Access, correction, deletion, and ads

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, receive a portable copy, or appeal a decision about your request.

To make a request, email support.meebugames@gmail.com with the subject “Data Privacy Request.” Tell us which game or Service you used and provide enough non-sensitive detail for us to locate the relevant information. We may need to verify that the request belongs to you.

You can also manage ad personalisation and advertising identifiers from your device settings. Menu names differ by device and operating-system version; look for controls under Privacy, Ads, or Google services.

08 / Security & international transfers

Protection across a global service

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Meebu and its providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use recognised safeguards for international transfers and require providers to protect information consistently with applicable law.

09 / Changes to this policy

When this page changes

We may update this policy when our games, providers, legal obligations, or privacy practices change. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and provide additional notice inside a Service when a change is material and the law requires it.
